# 🌍 Learn&Share — Collaborative Peer-to-Peer Learning Platform

**Learn&Share** is a community-driven platform that empowers people to both teach and learn real-life skills in a flexible, accessible, and engaging way. Whether you're a teenager learning guitar, a student improving your coding skills, or someone passionate about sharing your cooking expertise — Learn&Share connects learners and mentors across all areas of knowledge.

Unlike traditional educational platforms, Learn&Share promotes **peer-to-peer education**. Everyone can be both a teacher and a student. Our mission is to democratize learning by removing the financial and structural barriers to skill development.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📱 Mobile-first experience (React Native + Expo)
- 👤 Instructor and learner profiles
- 📅 Lesson booking via video calls
- ⭐ Real-time reviews and reputation system
- 💰 Wallet system for payments (in development)
- ✅ Optional verification for trusted mentors
- 🧩 Community-driven lesson rating
- 🏆 Badges and gamification features (planned)
